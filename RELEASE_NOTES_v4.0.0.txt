CHINUUU v4.0.0 — DURABLE INTELLIGENCE & OPERATIONAL RECOVERY

Major additions
- Conversation continuity summaries generated by the configured local Ollama model and stored in SQLite.
- Original chat messages are preserved; summarization failure never fails the user's request.
- Deterministic relevance ranking limits approved memories injected into the prompt.
- Stale agent runs older than ten minutes are marked interrupted on startup; actions are never replayed automatically.
- Read-only /health diagnostics cover SQLite integrity, workspace availability, configured Ollama model and registered tools.
- /context reports message counts, approved memories, pending suggestions and summary coverage.
- GUI includes a Health Check shortcut and handles /health and /context directly.

Migration and privacy
- Additive SQLite schema migration; existing conversation, memories, tasks, indexed documents, reminders, contacts and audit history are preserved.
- Conversation summaries use only the configured local Ollama endpoint. No cloud service is added.
- Original messages are never deleted by the summary process. Summaries are historical context, not executable instructions.
